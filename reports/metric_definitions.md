# CredResolve Collections Recovery Analytics

# Metric Definitions

## 1. Accounts Analyzed

**Metric:** `accounts_analyzed`

**Definition:** Number of unique account-level records in `account_features`.

**Calculation:**

    COUNT(*)

The account-level analytical dataset contains one record per account.

---

## 2. Recovered Accounts

**Metric:** `recovered_accounts`

**Definition:** Number of accounts with at least one valid SUCCESS payment after payment IDs have been deduplicated.

**Calculation:**

    SUM(recovered_account)

Where:

    recovered_account = 1

when the account has at least one deduplicated payment with:

    payment_status = 'SUCCESS'

---

## 3. Unrecovered Accounts

**Metric:** `unrecovered_accounts`

**Definition:** Accounts without a successful payment.

**Calculation:**

    accounts_analyzed - recovered_accounts

---

## 4. Recovery Rate

**Metric:** `recovery_rate`

**Definition:** Share of analyzed portfolio accounts that have been recovered.

**Calculation:**

    recovered_accounts / accounts_analyzed

For the current portfolio:

    13,284 / 30,000 = 44.28%

The reporting layer may express this as either a decimal or percentage.

---

## 5. Recovery Amount

**Metric:** `recovery_amount`

**Definition:** Total monetary recovery generated by valid SUCCESS payments only after payment records have been deduplicated at payment-ID level.

FAILED, PENDING, and REVERSED payments are excluded.

**Calculation:**

    SUM(amount)
    WHERE payment_status = 'SUCCESS'

after:

    one record per payment_id

The current authoritative portfolio recovery amount is:

    ₹1,315,583,964.64

approximately:

    ₹131.56 Cr

This is the authoritative recovery definition used throughout the downstream analysis.

---

## 6. Average Recovery per Recovered Account

**Metric:** `average_recovery_per_recovered_account`

**Definition:** Average SUCCESS-based recovery amount among recovered accounts.

**Calculation:**

    recovery_amount / recovered_accounts

Current portfolio value:

    ₹1,315,583,964.64 / 13,284

    = approximately ₹99,035.23

---

## 7. Outstanding Amount

**Metric:** `outstanding_amount`

**Definition:** Total outstanding balance represented by the analyzed accounts.

**Calculation:**

    SUM(outstanding_amount)

Current portfolio outstanding amount:

    approximately ₹10,489,040,000

---

## 8. Recovery-to-Outstanding Ratio

**Metric:** `recovery_to_outstanding_ratio`

**Definition:** SUCCESS-based recovery amount relative to total outstanding portfolio balance.

**Calculation:**

    SUM(SUCCESS payment amount)
    /
    SUM(outstanding_amount)

Only SUCCESS payment amounts are included in the numerator.

# Monthly Recovery Metrics

## 31. Monthly Recovered Accounts

**Metric:** `monthly_recovered_accounts`

**Definition:** Number of distinct accounts with at least one SUCCESS payment during the month after payment-ID deduplication.

**Calculation:**

    COUNT(DISTINCT account_id)
    WHERE payment_status = 'SUCCESS'

---

## 32. Monthly Recovery Amount

**Metric:** `monthly_recovery_amount`

**Definition:** Total SUCCESS payment amount during the month after payment-ID deduplication.

**Calculation:**

    SUM(amount)
    WHERE payment_status = 'SUCCESS'

---

## 33. Worked-Account Recovery Rate

**Metric:** `worked_account_recovery_rate_pct`

**Definition:** Recovered accounts divided by accounts with payment activity during the month.

**Calculation:**

    recovered_accounts
    /
    accounts_with_payment_activity

This denominator represents only accounts with payment activity.

It must not be directly compared with the portfolio recovery rate without acknowledging the denominator difference.

---

## 34. Portfolio Recovery Rate

**Metric:** `portfolio_recovery_rate_pct`

**Definition:** Monthly recovered accounts divided by the total portfolio account population.

**Calculation:**

    recovered_accounts
    /
    total_portfolio_accounts

---

## 35. Recovery Amount MoM Change

**Metric:** `recovery_amount_mom_pct`

**Definition:** Percentage change in SUCCESS-based recovery amount compared with the previous comparable month.

**Calculation:**

    ((current_month_recovery_amount
      - previous_month_recovery_amount)
      /
      previous_month_recovery_amount)
    * 100

---

## 36. Recovered Accounts MoM Change

**Metric:** `recovered_accounts_mom_pct`

**Definition:** Percentage change in recovered accounts compared with the previous comparable month.

**Calculation:**

    ((current_month_recovered_accounts
      - previous_month_recovered_accounts)
      /
      previous_month_recovered_accounts)
    * 100

---

# Recovery Exposure Analysis

## 38. Recovery by Exposure

Recovery rates by:

- Calls
- Attempts
- Agents
- Campaigns
- Vendors
- Targeting
- Call direction
- Calling time

are descriptive comparisons across exposure groups.

These metrics represent observed associations.

They should **not** be interpreted as causal estimates of the effect of collection activity.

For example:

    Higher call exposure
    ≠
    Calls caused higher recovery

Collection exposure may be influenced by borrower risk, DPD, account status, portfolio mix, targeting rules, or other operational factors.

---

# Statistical Interpretation

## 39. Association Versus Causation

Statistical tests and regression models in this project identify relationships between observed variables and recovery outcomes.

A statistically significant association does not prove that the underlying operational variable caused the recovery outcome.

Causal claims require an appropriate experimental or quasi-experimental design.

The project therefore reports:

    Association ≠ Causation

for observational driver analysis.

# Metric Availability Principle

Where a required business metric cannot be reliably calculated from the supplied data:

    NOT ESTIMABLE FROM SUPPLIED DATA

must be reported instead of inventing a value.

This principle applies particularly to:

- Cost per ₹ Recovered
- Any metric requiring unavailable operational cost data
- Any causal effect that cannot be identified from the observational dataset

The absence of a metric is itself an analytical finding when the required source field is unavailable.
